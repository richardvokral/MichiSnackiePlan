# Vision — Michi Snackie Plan

**Easy lean setup.** Eating lean and healthy should take seconds of decision-making a day, not spreadsheets.

## The idea

Michi Snackie Plan suggests five sensible meals a day — breakfast, morning snack, lunch, afternoon snack, dinner — from a curated catalog of healthy, protein-aware meals. The app does the thinking: variety, dietary rules, and reasonable portions are built into the recommendation engine, so the user just picks (or accepts) and eats.

## Target user

One person (or a household) who wants to eat lean without becoming a nutrition accountant. They open the app in the morning, see today's plan, maybe swap a meal, done.

## Principles

1. **Low-friction daily choice.** The default path is "open the app, accept today's plan." Every extra tap must earn its place.
2. **Variety without thinking.** The engine avoids repeats, alternates styles, and rotates proteins and carb bases so meals stay interesting without manual planning.
3. **Lean-aware, not obsessive.** Calories and protein are visible and guide the suggestions, but the app is a gentle guide — not a rigorous tracking tool.
4. **Curated over infinite.** A well-maintained catalog (hand-edited + AI-generated, USDA-grounded nutrition) beats an endless recipe database.
5. **Calm UX.** Mobile-first, quiet visuals, no gamification, no guilt.

## Non-goals

- Not a precise calorie/macro tracker (no barcode scanning, no logging every bite).
- No social features, feeds, or sharing.
- No cooking-instruction depth — meals are simple enough that ingredients + portions suffice.
- No multi-tenant/commercial ambitions; this is a personal/household tool.
